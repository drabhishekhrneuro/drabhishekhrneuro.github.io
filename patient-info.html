Understanding Your Condition Is the First Step

Neurosurgery deals with conditions affecting the brain, spine, spinal cord and nerves. While some conditions require surgery or an intervention, not every condition needs an operation.

Surgery is not always the answer

An abnormality on an MRI or CT scan does not automatically mean that surgery is required. Treatment decisions depend on several factors, including your symptoms, clinical examination, scan findings, the severity of the condition and how it is likely to behave over time.

When surgery or an intervention is necessary, the goal is to choose the right treatment for the right patient and, whenever appropriate and safe, use a minimally invasive approach. The aim is not simply to treat what is visible on a scan, but to use the holistic approach and treat the person and the problem as a whole.
