Medicine is complicated enough. Understanding it shouldn't be.

I am Dr. Abhishek H R, a neurosurgeon based in Bengaluru, with training and experience in brain, spine and neurovascular surgery.

Neurosurgical decisions can be frightening for patients and families. My approach is to explain the problem clearly, discuss the available options and help patients understand why a particular treatment may or may not be recommended.

Alongside clinical practice, I am passionate about patient education — breaking down complicated neurological topics into information that ordinary people can actually understand.

Learn. Understand. Decide.

Patient Education

Brain scans, slipped discs, strokes, aneurysms, headaches and brain surgery can sound terrifying — especially when explained in medical jargon. I create educational content to make neurological and neurosurgical topics easier to understand.
