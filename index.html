Spine Surgery

Stroke & Neurointervention

Conditions

Conditions I Treat

Common neurological and neurosurgical conditions that may require specialist assessment or treatment.

Brain Tumours

Evaluation and surgical management of selected brain tumours.

Head Injury

Assessment and surgical care of traumatic brain injuries.

Brain Haemorrhage

Management of intracranial bleeding and related neurosurgical conditions.

Stroke

Evaluation and appropriate intervention in selected stroke patients.

Brain Aneurysms

Assessment and treatment of selected cerebral aneurysms.

Spine Disorders

Surgical treatment of selected degenerative and traumatic spine conditions.

Disc Problems

Assessment and treatment of symptomatic spinal disc disease.

Spinal Stenosis

Evaluation of narrowing affecting the spinal canal or neural structures.

Vascular Conditions of the Brain

Evaluation of selected conditions affecting cerebral blood vessels.

About

Dr. Abhishek H R

I am a neurosurgeon based in Bengaluru with clinical interests spanning brain surgery, spine surgery, stroke and neurointervention.

My approach is centred around understanding the patient's condition, explaining the available treatment options clearly and helping patients and families make informed decisions.

Alongside clinical practice, I have a strong interest in patient education and communicating neurological and neurosurgical topics in a way that is accessible to everyone.
